Social Accounts Vault keeps your social media accounts, websites and apps
organised in one place — on your own device, and nowhere else.

• Bold, colourful cards for every email you use
• Tap a card and a panel slides up: every Facebook, Instagram and TikTok
  ID on that email, each with its flag and country — plus any website
  (site name + URL) and any other app, all under the same email
• Facebook Pages listed under each Facebook ID (page name + followers)
• Passwords with show/hide and one-tap copy
• LOGIN buttons open each platform's login page in your browser; website
  entries open their site, app entries open the installed app (or a Play
  Store search when it is not installed)
• PIN lock with fingerprint unlock — the vault locks itself when it goes
  to the background
• Password generator and strength meter, live 2FA codes (TOTP) per entry,
  duplicate-password warnings, categories, notes and recovery details
• Backup and restore your data as a JSON file; dark mode
• Add, edit and delete entries (delete asks twice), filter by Social /
  Websites / Apps, and search by email, username, country, site, app,
  notes or category

Privacy first: everything is stored only in a local database on your
phone. The app asks for no network permission at all — no internet
access, no contacts, no storage — and sends nothing anywhere. It starts
empty; you add your own accounts inside the app. The backup file holds
your passwords in readable form, so keep it private and never share it.
